About this model

Inspired by the five plane approach in the supplied reference: privileged access, control, management, data/workloads, and user/application access. The tool is intentionally scoped to Power Platform solutions and systems integrated with them. A component may have responsibilities across multiple planes; the selected plane is its primary depiction in this view.

Use sample architectures as illustrative starting points, not as Microsoft-certified architectures or implementation guidance.

Network transparency

Architectures are processed in your browser and are not sent to a service. Export a JSON copy to preserve your work; importing replaces the current diagram.

To verify network traffic: press F12 → Network → Preserve log → reload the page. An in-page list is not a substitute for Developer Tools or independent analysis.
